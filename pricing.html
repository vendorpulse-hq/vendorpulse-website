Frequently asked.

The questions every vendor asks us during their first call.

Why don't you publish a price like normal SaaS companies?
Because vendors are not normal SaaS customers. A vendor running one ceramic-coating bay at a single Maruti dealership and a vendor running PPF, ceramic, and detailing across 20 Mahindra and Tata locations are entirely different businesses. Listing one price would either be too expensive for the first or too cheap for the second. A 10-minute call is faster than a pricing page filled with footnotes.
What does a typical month look like?
For most vendors, VendorPulse costs less per month than the time you currently lose to manual reconciliation. We've designed it so that even at our higher pricing tier, the ROI shows up in the first month — saved supervisor time, faster customer turnaround, eliminated damage disputes. Ask us during the call and we'll show you the math on your specific numbers.
Is there a setup fee?
No setup fees. We handle onboarding personally — including user creation, treatment catalogue setup, branding customisation, and a hands-on session with your team. That's part of the subscription.
Do I have to commit for a year?
No. Month-to-month is fine. We offer a discount for annual commitments, but it's optional. We're confident the product earns its place every month — if it doesn't, you should be free to leave.
Can I try it before paying?
Yes. We typically set up a 2-4 week pilot with one location, one supervisor, and one or two service advisers. You see the real workflows on real data. If it works, we expand to your full operation. If not, no hard feelings.
What if my dealership partner says no?
The vendor side of VendorPulse works completely standalone — you can run all your bookings, photos, attendance, and reports without dealership involvement. Adding dealership-side users (Service Managers viewing live job status) makes the platform stronger, but it's never required. Most of our early pilots run on the vendor side only.
What happens to my data if I cancel?
You own your data. We give you full Excel and PDF exports of every booking, photo, and report on request — at any time, for any reason. After cancellation, we keep your data for 90 days in case you change your mind, then delete it permanently.
What languages does it work in?
The platform is currently in English. Hindi, Telugu, and Tamil are on the roadmap based on customer demand. For field staff, we've kept the UI heavily icon-driven and gesture-based so language is less of a barrier than in typical enterprise apps. We're happy to walk you through this with your team during the demo.
