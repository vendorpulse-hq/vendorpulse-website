VendorPulse covers the entire vendor operation — from the moment a service advisor creates a booking at the dealership counter to the final reconciled report at month end. Here's how each module fits together.

Six modules.
One unified workflow.

Each module talks to the others. Create a booking, photos auto-link to it. Deliver the booking, incentives auto-calculate. Generate a report, the data is already there.

Bookings

Multi-treatment bookings with customer details, vehicle info, smart pricing, and approval flows for discounts.

Photo documentation

Live tracking

Real-time job status across all locations. Owners see everything; supervisors see their bay; advisors see their bookings.

Attendance

GPS-stamped check-in/check-out for supervisors and applicators. Geofenced to the dealership boundary.

Incentives

01 / Booking management

From walk-in to delivery,
everything is captured.

Service advisors create bookings in under a minute. Multi-treatment selection, smart pricing, customer phone numbers, delivery expectations — all in one place. The Supervisor team gets a push notification the instant a booking is created.

  • ✓Multi-treatment selection with auto-calculated pricing
  • ✓Customer & vehicle details with one-tap call buttons
  • ✓Discount and No-Demand-Promise (NDP) approval workflows
  • ✓Status-based filters: Booked, Accepted, In Progress, QC, Delivered
  • ✓Cancellation flows with mandatory reasons for audit
02 / Photo documentation

17-area capture.
GPS-stamped. Dispute-proof.

Every job starts with a structured photo capture covering 17 distinct vehicle areas — bonnet, front bumper, doors, wheel arches, interior. GPS coordinates are embedded in every photo's metadata. Try to upload from gallery? Blocked. Try to fake the location? Rejected. The evidence is bulletproof.

  • ✓Structured 17-area capture flow — no missed angles
  • ✓GPS within 500m of dealership, time-stamped, vendor-stamped
  • ✓Camera-only — gallery upload blocked for integrity
  • ✓Customer can review every photo before delivery
  • ✓Cloud-stored on R2, accessible from any device, forever
03 / Live job tracking

Stop calling for status updates.
Watch them live.

Owners see every job at every location, in real time. Supervisors see their bay's queue. Applicators see only what's assigned to them. Status changes propagate instantly via WebSocket — no refresh needed, no WhatsApp pings to chase.

  • ✓Live booking list with status colour-coding
  • ✓Multi-location filtering for owners and managers
  • ✓Quality check gates before delivery — no skipping QC
  • ✓Push notifications routed by role and relevance
  • ✓Audit trail: who changed what status, when, why
04 / Attendance & incentives

Two workflows.
One operations engine.

Attendance is GPS-geofenced — supervisors and applicators must check in within 100m of the dealership. Incentives are slab-based and retroactive — when a service advisor crosses the next tier on their fifth booking, all four previous bookings instantly recalculate at the new rate. No manual spreadsheet adjustments.

  • ✓GPS check-in/check-out with 100m geofence
  • ✓Daily attendance history and monthly summaries
  • ✓Slab-based incentive calculator with retroactive logic
  • ✓Configurable tiers per role — SA, supervisor, applicator
  • ✓Approval workflows for late check-ins or overrides
05 / Analytics & reports

Numbers you can trust.
Reports you didn't have to build.

A custom report builder with 22 fields across four groups. Pick what you need, schedule it, get it in your inbox every Monday morning. No more building Excel pivots at midnight. The owner dashboard shows revenue, discounts, applicator productivity, and attendance — all calculated automatically.

  • ✓Owner dashboard with real-time KPIs
  • ✓Custom report builder — 22 fields, four groups
  • ✓Scheduled daily, weekly, or monthly auto-delivery
  • ✓Export in PDF, Excel, or CSV
  • ✓Discount impact analytics and approval breakdowns
