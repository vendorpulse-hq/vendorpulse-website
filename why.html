Four things only VendorPulse does.

The capabilities that come from being built specifically for this industry, not retrofitted from a generic platform.

Treatment-aware booking model

Most CRMs see a booking as a generic "deal". We model it as a vehicle with multiple treatments, each with its own pricing, supervisor approval, applicator assignment, before/after photos, and quality check. A ceramic-coating booking is a different beast from a PPF booking, and the platform knows it.

Dispute-proof field documentation

Our photo system is camera-only (no gallery uploads), GPS-validated within 500m of the dealership, time-stamped, and tied to the booking forever. When the dealership claims you scratched the bumper, you have 17 angles taken before work began, with verifiable metadata. Every other tool relies on WhatsApp screenshots.

Retroactive incentive calculation

A service advisor crosses from 3 to 4 bookings — under our model, all four bookings instantly recalculate at the new slab rate. No spreadsheet. No manual adjustment. No "I'll fix it next month." This is operationally how Indian vendor incentives actually work, and nobody else encodes it natively.

Dual-sided visibility, single source of truth

Vendor users see vendor data. Dealership users see dealership-relevant data. But both look at the same bookings, same statuses, same photos. No more weekly Excel reconciliation between "their numbers" and "your numbers". The numbers are everyone's.
