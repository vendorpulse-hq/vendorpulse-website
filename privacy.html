Privacy Policy

What we collect, why we collect it, and what we do with it. Written in plain language — because if you can't understand it, it isn't really informed consent.

Last updated · 17 June 2026
The short version VendorPulse is a B2B operations platform. We collect data needed to run your vendor business — bookings, photos, attendance, customer details — and share it only with the vendor and the dealership in your specific business relationship. We do not sell your data, we do not share it with advertisers, and we do not use it to train AI models. You can export or delete it at any time.

1. Who this policy applies to

This Privacy Policy covers data processed through:

  • The VendorPulse mobile application on Android and iOS
  • The VendorPulse web-based administration interface
  • This website (vendorpulse.in) and the contact form on it

It applies to all users of the platform — vendor owners, vendor managers, supervisors, applicators, service advisors, service managers, and anyone else with an account — as well as visitors to this website.

2. Who is responsible for your data

VendorPulse is operated from India by Kamalan Vankayala. For the purposes of the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDPA), VendorPulse acts as:

  • Data Fiduciary for account information of users, website visitor data, and operational platform data
  • Data Processor for end-customer information (such as the names and vehicle details of customers booked into the platform by your team), which the vendor business is the Data Fiduciary for

You can reach our point of contact for any data-related question at kamalan.vankayala@vendorpulse.in.

3. What data we collect

3.1 Account information

When your administrator creates your VendorPulse account, we receive and store:

  • Your full name
  • Email address
  • Mobile number (if provided)
  • Your role (Service Advisor, Supervisor, Applicator, etc.)
  • Your assigned location and organisation (vendor or OEM dealership)
  • Date of birth (optional, only if you provide it)
  • A securely hashed copy of your password (we never store passwords in plain text)

3.2 Operational data you create or interact with

As you use the platform, we collect data that records your work:

  • Bookings — customer name, vehicle make, model and registration number, treatment type, pricing, status, and notes
  • Photos — before and after photos of vehicles you capture during job documentation, with embedded EXIF metadata (date, time, and GPS coordinates of capture)
  • Attendance — your check-in and check-out times, with the GPS coordinates of where each check-in or check-out occurred
  • Incentives — the bookings credited to you, calculated commission amounts, and approval history
  • Audit trail — a record of significant actions you take in the system (creating a booking, approving a discount, etc.) for security and dispute resolution

3.3 Location data

VendorPulse uses your device's GPS location in three specific situations only:

  • When you check in or check out for attendance (to verify presence within the dealership's geofence, typically a 100-metre radius)
  • When you capture a vehicle photo during job documentation (to embed location proof inside the photo)
  • When you accept or update a booking (to validate the action originated from a permitted location)

We do not track your location continuously. We do not collect location data when the app is in the background. We do not build any kind of movement profile, route history, or behaviour pattern.

3.4 Device and technical data

To deliver the service reliably and keep accounts secure, we automatically collect:

  • Device type, operating system, and operating system version
  • Push notification token (for sending booking alerts and approval requests)
  • Approximate IP address and the timestamp of each session, used only for security monitoring (e.g. detecting unusual sign-in patterns)
  • Failed login attempts and account-lock events

3.5 Website data

This website (vendorpulse.in) is currently kept deliberately lightweight. We do not run any third-party analytics, advertising, retargeting, or behavioural tracking on this site. The only data we collect from website visitors is what you choose to send through the contact form on the demo request page — your name, company, email, phone, and the message you write. That submission goes to our team's inbox through Formspree and is used to respond to you.

4. How we use your data

We use the data described above for the following purposes, and only these:

  • Running the service — authenticating your sign-in, displaying your bookings, allowing your supervisor to see job status, calculating incentives, generating reports, sending push notifications relevant to your role
  • Operational accountability — making before/after photo evidence available to your vendor, the dealership, and the end customer for dispute resolution
  • Security — detecting suspicious sign-in attempts, locking accounts after repeated failures, maintaining audit logs
  • Customer support — responding to questions you send us
  • Legal compliance — meeting tax, accounting, or law-enforcement obligations when properly served with valid legal process
  • Improving the product — reviewing aggregated, anonymised patterns of how the app is used (for example, which screens cause the most exits) to fix friction and add useful features

We will not use your data for any other purpose without telling you first and obtaining your consent.

5. What we do NOT do with your data

To be explicit about boundaries we have set ourselves:

  • We do not sell your data to anyone, ever, for any reason
  • We do not share your data with advertisers, marketing networks, data brokers, or aggregators
  • We do not use your photos, booking data, or any other content to train artificial intelligence or machine learning models
  • We do not give third-party developers access to your data through APIs without your explicit administrator's authorisation
  • We do not match data across vendors — each vendor's data is isolated and never combined for cross-vendor benchmarking without explicit consent

6. Who sees your data

Your data is visible only to specific parties with a legitimate need to see it:

  • Your vendor organisation — owners and managers within your vendor company can see the operational data of users on their team, scoped by location and role permissions
  • The dealership where you work — service managers at an OEM dealership can see the bookings and job status that occur at their location, but cannot see vendor-internal data like applicator salaries, supervisor attendance details, or commission calculations
  • The end customer — the car owner whose vehicle is being treated can be shown the before/after photos of their own vehicle at the vendor's discretion
  • VendorPulse staff — limited to the founder and any future authorised support team members, only when investigating a support ticket or a security incident, and access is logged
  • Service providers we use — see the next section

7. Service providers and where data is stored

To deliver VendorPulse, we rely on a small number of carefully chosen infrastructure providers. Each has been selected for its security and privacy posture. We do not pass data to any other third party.

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Germany) — hosts the VendorPulse application servers and the PostgreSQL database where your bookings, account information, and operational records are stored
  • Cloudflare, Inc. (United States) — provides DNS, the public website at vendorpulse.in, and our R2 object storage where photos uploaded from the mobile app are stored
  • Resend (United States) — handles transactional emails such as account invites, password resets, and report deliveries
  • Zoho Corporation (India) — provides our team email infrastructure
  • Formspree (United States) — receives submissions from the contact form on this website and forwards them to our inbox
  • Expo / Apple / Google — operate the app distribution and push notification infrastructure for the mobile apps

Some of these providers are located outside India. By using VendorPulse, you consent to the cross-border transfer of your data to these jurisdictions, where it is protected by each provider's own data protection standards and by the contractual safeguards we maintain with them.

8. How long we keep your data

  • While your account is active — we retain all data for as long as you remain an active user of the platform
  • After deactivation or cancellation — we retain data for 90 days after a vendor cancels their VendorPulse subscription, to give you time to change your mind or request exports. After 90 days, the data is permanently deleted, except for data we are legally required to keep longer (such as financial transaction records required by Indian tax law, retained for 7 years)
  • Audit logs and security records — retained for 12 months for security investigation purposes, then deleted
  • Contact form submissions — retained in our team inbox for as long as needed to respond, then archived or deleted

9. Your rights

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and as a matter of our own policy, you have the right to:

  • Access your data — request a complete copy of all data we hold about you, in a portable format (PDF or Excel)
  • Correct your data — ask us to fix any inaccurate or outdated information
  • Delete your data — request that we delete your data, except where we are legally required to keep it
  • Withdraw consent — withdraw any consent you have given for data processing that depends on consent rather than another legal basis
  • Nominate a representative — appoint someone else to exercise these rights on your behalf in the event you cannot
  • Lodge a complaint — file a grievance directly with us or, if unresolved, with the Data Protection Board of India

To exercise any of these rights, email kamalan.vankayala@vendorpulse.in. We will respond within 30 days. There is no charge for any of these requests under normal circumstances.

10. Security

We take security seriously and apply standard, sensible engineering protections:

  • All data in transit between your device and our servers is encrypted using TLS 1.2 or higher
  • Passwords are stored using bcrypt one-way hashing — we cannot see or recover your password, even if asked
  • Sessions use short-lived access tokens combined with rotating refresh tokens
  • Accounts lock automatically after 5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts
  • Photos are stored in private, access-controlled storage with signed time-limited download URLs
  • The mobile app supports biometric (fingerprint or Face ID) sign-in for an additional security layer

No system is perfectly secure. If we ever detect a breach affecting your personal data, we will notify you and the Data Protection Board of India in accordance with the timelines required by law.

11. Children's data

VendorPulse is a business-to-business platform intended for professional adults employed by automotive treatment vendors and their OEM dealership partners. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under the age of 18. If you believe a child's data has been collected by us inadvertently, please contact us immediately and we will delete it.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy occasionally — for example, when we add a new feature that requires new types of data, or when a service provider changes. When we make a material change, we will:

  • Update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page
  • Send an in-app notification to all active users describing what changed and why
  • For substantial changes, also send an email to the registered email address on each account

Continuing to use the platform after a change to this policy constitutes acceptance of the updated terms. If you do not agree, you may exercise your right to delete your data and discontinue use.

13. Contact us

Privacy questions, data requests, and grievances can be directed to our point of contact:

  • Email: kamalan.vankayala@vendorpulse.in
  • Subject line: begin with [Privacy] for faster triage
  • Postal: available on request

We will acknowledge your message within 3 business days and respond substantively within 30 days. Thank you for reading.
