Indian automotive treatment vendors run a ₹3,200 crore industry on paper, WhatsApp, and trust. We're building the missing piece — one vendor at a time.

Why VendorPulse exists.

Walk into any car dealership in India and you'll find a vendor running a real business in the back. Skilled supervisors. Trained applicators. Service advisors managing multi-lakh monthly bookings. These vendors serve dealership chains, employ teams across multiple locations, reconcile bills with OEM accounts, calculate incentives, and track attendance — every single day, at scale.

And almost none of it lives in software. WhatsApp groups carry the bookings. Excel sheets track the revenue. Physical registers log the attendance. Every dispute over a scratched bumper goes against the vendor because there's no photo proof. Every month closes with two or three days of manual reconciliation between vendor records and the dealership's DMS exports.

VendorPulse is the operating system for this industry. Not a generic field-service app retrofitted to fit. Not a CRM that doesn't understand what a Service Advisor at the counter actually does. A platform built specifically — and only — for car treatment vendors and the dealerships they serve.

What we believe

Three convictions that shape
every product decision.

1

Field-first, not desk-first

Most B2B SaaS is built for someone at a desk. Our users are at a service bay holding a polishing tool. We design for that reality first, and let the desk view follow.

2

Vertical depth over horizontal breadth

We're not trying to be the platform for "all field service businesses". We're specifically the platform for automotive treatment vendors. Depth beats breadth.

3

Honesty in pricing and promises

We don't quote fake metrics. We don't fake customer logos. We don't oversell. If we say a feature ships in two weeks, it ships in two weeks. Or we tell you why.
